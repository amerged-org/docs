title: "Support"
description: "Tell your agent what you need. It reports bugs, issues and feature requests to ohmyho.st and follows up on them."
---

Support runs through your coding agent. Describe the problem or the feature you need in plain words; the agent sends ohmyho.st a redacted report through the MCP server or the CLI and gives you a receipt ID.

## Ask your agent

```text
My Prod deploy of shop-app failed. Report it to ohmyho.st support and give me the receipt ID.
```

```text
Send ohmyho.st a feature request: exports straight to my own S3 bucket.
```

The agent files a `bug`, an `issue` (something looks wrong but is not confirmed) or a `feature_request` with MCP `feedback_submit` or `ohmyhost feedback submit`. It adds the workspace, project and operation IDs and the error code, and leaves out tokens, passwords, raw logs and customer records. Reports cost no credits and are never published.

## Follow up

Keep the receipt ID and ask for the status whenever you want an update:

```text
What is the status of my ohmyho.st report <receipt ID>?
```

The agent reads `feedback_status`: `received`, `in_review`, `planned`, `in_progress`, `resolved` with the release that contains the fix, or `closed` with an explanation. Replies from ohmyho.st appear there. You are not notified by email, and there is no list of past reports. [Report fields and status details](/feedback).

## When your agent cannot report

A report needs a signed-in agent with access to your workspace. Use the [contact form](https://ohmyho.st/contact) if you cannot sign in, if a billing error tells you to contact support, or for privacy and DPA questions.
