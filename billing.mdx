[Billing](https://app.ohmyho.st/billing) shows effective Free/Paid access, monthly credits with their expiry and non-expiring one-time credits. A Beta badge means Paid features were granted; it does not represent a Stripe subscription or add another monthly Paid allowance.

## Make a purchase

```sh
ohmyhost billing checkout --organization "$ORGANIZATION_ID" --offer paid --idempotency-key "$CHECKOUT_REQUEST_KEY" --json
```

Use `--offer topup --packs 1` for a one-time credit purchase. Open the returned human Checkout URL, review the total and complete payment yourself. The command does not pay automatically. Every successful purchase has an invoice; applicable tax is calculated by Stripe Tax.

Follow the returned checkout ID with `billing status`. A top-up adds credits and does not extend a subscription. Payment availability is reported by your account; do not treat a disabled Live handoff as a completed purchase.

## Auto-recharge

When enabled, a refill adds **1,000 non-expiring credits for USD 9 plus tax** after the available balance falls below 100 credits. You explicitly authorize recurring off-session charges and choose a gross UTC-month limit including tax.

```sh
ohmyhost billing recharge get --organization "$ORGANIZATION_ID" --json
```

To enable, use the returned policy revision, a new retained key, your selected limit in USD cents and the required consent:

```sh
ohmyhost billing recharge set --organization "$ORGANIZATION_ID" --enabled true --monthly-limit-minor "$MONTHLY_LIMIT_CENTS" --revision "$POLICY_REVISION" --idempotency-key "$RECHARGE_REQUEST_KEY" --consent off_session_v1 --json
```

Complete the returned card-setup URL if needed, then read policy and payment readiness again. Consent must come from the organization Owner; a website pricing switch is not payment authorization.

To turn it off, read the latest revision and submit `--enabled false` with that revision and a new key. Reuse the current saved monthly limit. Turning it off prevents new attempts; an already submitted payment can still settle.

Only one unresolved refill is allowed. A tax/address/payment issue requires the stated action; do not create a second payment to bypass it or disable tax. Credits are issued from confirmed paid events, once.

## Manage payment details

```sh
ohmyhost billing portal --organization "$ORGANIZATION_ID" --json
```

Open the fresh returned Stripe Portal URL to manage available billing settings. Keep expired signed links out of saved project notes. [Usage](/usage) · [Budgets](/budgets).
