---
title: "Compute performance unavailable"
description: "What the `compute_performance_unavailable` error means and what to do next."
---

The API, CLI and MCP return `compute_performance_unavailable` with HTTP 503. Retrying the same request unchanged does not help.

## What to do

Choose standard compute or report inactive performance pricing through feedback. Do not repeatedly resubmit.

Keep the `request_id` from the error when you [report the problem](/feedback).
