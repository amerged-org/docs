---
title: "Powered by flag required"
description: "What the `powered_by_flag_required` error means and what to do next."
---

The API, CLI and MCP return `powered_by_flag_required` with HTTP 409. Retrying the same request unchanged does not help.

## What to do

Keep the flag on, remove the custom domain with 'ohmyhost domain paid delete', or buy Paid, then switch the flag off.

Keep the `request_id` from the error when you [report the problem](/feedback).
