---
title: "Billing purchase conflict"
description: "What the `billing_purchase_conflict` error means and what to do next."
---

The API, CLI and MCP return `billing_purchase_conflict` with HTTP 409. Retrying the same request unchanged does not help.

## What to do

Read the original checkout and use billing portal to manage the existing subscription. Do not create another purchase after uncertainty.

Keep the `request_id` from the error when you [report the problem](/feedback).
