---
title: "Project handle invalid"
description: "What the `project_handle_invalid` error means and what to do next."
---

The API, CLI and MCP return `project_handle_invalid` with HTTP 409. Repeating the same request unchanged does not help.

## What to do

Choose one to five lowercase words of letters and digits, three to fifty-nine characters, avoiding reserved words.

If the steps above do not resolve it, [report it](/feedback) with the `request_id` from the error.
