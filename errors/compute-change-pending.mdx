---
title: "Compute change pending"
description: "What the `compute_change_pending` error means and what to do next."
---

The API, CLI and MCP return `compute_change_pending` with HTTP 409. Retrying the same request unchanged does not help.

## What to do

Poll the existing operation after 60 seconds. Do not submit another size change, deletion or transfer while it runs.

Keep the `request_id` from the error when you [report the problem](/feedback).
