---
title: "Compute performance paid required"
description: "What the `compute_performance_paid_required` error means and what to do next."
---

The API, CLI and MCP return `compute_performance_paid_required` with HTTP 403. Retrying the same request unchanged does not help.

## What to do

Check Paid service and usable credits or grace, or choose standard compute.

Keep the `request_id` from the error when you [report the problem](/feedback).
