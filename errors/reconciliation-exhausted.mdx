---
title: "Reconciliation exhausted"
description: "What the `reconciliation_exhausted` error means and what to do next."
---

The API, CLI and MCP return `reconciliation_exhausted` with HTTP 409. Repeating the same request unchanged does not help.

## What to do

Stop retries and submit feedback with this operation ID. A platform recovery review is required.

If the steps above do not resolve it, [report it](/feedback) with the `request_id` from the error.
