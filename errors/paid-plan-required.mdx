---
title: "Paid plan required"
description: "What the `paid_plan_required` error means and what to do next."
---

The API, CLI and MCP return `paid_plan_required` with HTTP 402. Retrying the same request unchanged does not help.

## What to do

Ask the organization Owner to activate Paid through billing checkout, then retry. Top-ups and signup credits do not activate Paid.

Keep the `request_id` from the error when you [report the problem](/feedback).
