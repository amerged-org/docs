---
title: "Understand usage"
description: "Read measured monthly consumption and the shared organization balance."
---

```sh
ohmyhost credits account --organization "$ORGANIZATION_ID" --json
ohmyhost credits usage --organization "$ORGANIZATION_ID" --month "$UTC_MONTH" --json
```

Use `UTC_MONTH` in `YYYY-MM` format. The account reports available credits and applicable rates; usage reports measured consumption. Follow the returned cursor to read subsequent pages.

## What contributes to consumption

Builds, active database compute, storage and supported service operations are measured through their actual meters. [Published credit prices](/pricing) list each measured quantity and its rate. Quiet databases can suspend compute, while retained storage/history still costs credits. A query may wake a suspended database.

Performance database pricing changes the database-compute rate. Do not multiply the complete bill or already valued usage again.

## Read the balance correctly

Free monthly credits expire on the date shown in Billing. Purchased credits (Paid periods, top-ups and recharges) and applicable signup grants never expire. Internal reservations protect in-flight work and remain available in the API; portal Billing and Usage keep the display simple.

Some measurements arrive later than the original operation. A low current total is not a guarantee that all running work has settled. [Project budgets](/budgets) control the intended monthly limit and its Continue/Stop behavior.
